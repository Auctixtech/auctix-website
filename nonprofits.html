What's included

Built for the way nonprofits actually run

Donor CRM

People, organizations and households as donors. Soft credits, matching and tribute gifts, relationship managers, and pipelines for major gifts, grants and sponsorships.

Fund accounting

A nonprofit chart of accounts, funds on every entry, program, management and fundraising classes, and the statements your auditor and Form 990 need.

Receipts and year-end statements

IRS wording, deductible amounts and in-kind descriptions handled for you. Issue in bulk, email from your own domain, or print.

Donation requests and online giving

Send sponsors a request with your own title and branding. They pay online by card or bank and the gift is recorded automatically.

Events and volunteers

Goals, sponsors, shifts and groups, a public sign-up page with capacity limits, attendance, and supplies planned from inventory.

Board dashboard

Revenue, expenses, net assets, months of cash and program expense ratio, with a view-only role for board members.

Expenses and bills

Volunteers and staff submit receipts from their phones; you approve, code to a fund and reimburse. Bills and vendor payments match the bank feed.

Email campaigns and inbox

Target donors by giving history, send from your domain with one-click unsubscribe, and keep every reply on the donor's timeline.

For food-packing organizations

Run a Kids Against Hunger location? The Meal Planner is free.

Built with Heartland Kids Against Hunger, our founding customer. Pick a formula and a meal count and AUCTIX works out every ingredient and packaging item, nets it against your stock and lots, and prints the pull sheet and order sheet.

Inventory and lots

Track items by location and lot with expiry dates, low-stock alerts and soonest-expiring-first pulls for every event.
