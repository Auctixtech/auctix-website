Questions

Do I still need QuickBooks?

No. AUCTIX keeps your books itself, with a full double-entry ledger, bank feeds, reconciliation and reports. Books are included on every plan. Your accountant can work in it directly.

Who holds the money when someone pays me?

You do. Payments go straight to your own Stripe account and Stripe pays out to your bank. You can connect the Stripe account you already have.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans are month to month. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the month you've paid for.

How does the founding offer work?

The first five organizations in each edition pay half price for their first 12 months. In return we ask for a short case study and a few introductions.

Can you move my data over?

Yes. We help bring in your contacts, history and opening balances as part of setup.
