The platform

Everything runs on one set of books

Every invoice, payment, bill and expense lands in your own double-entry ledger as it happens. No syncing, no exports, no month-end guessing.

Real accounting

Chart of accounts, bank feeds with rules and matching, bills, reconciliation, closing dates and financial reports.

CRM

People, organizations and households, a full activity timeline, tasks and opportunity pipelines.

Invoices and payments

Send an invoice, get paid by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or bank transfer into your own Stripe account, and the books update themselves.

Expenses

Snap a receipt and AI reads the merchant, date and total. Mileage, approvals and reimbursement in one flow.

Inventory and lots

Items, locations, lots with expiry dates and low-stock alerts. Events and production runs pull supplies automatically.

Inbox and email campaigns

A shared inbox that files mail to the right contact, templates, and campaigns sent from your own domain.

AI built in

Upload a PDF bank statement or a receipt photo and AI turns it into reviewed, balanced entries. You approve everything.

Reports and dashboards

Profit and loss, balance sheet, trial balance and an owner or board dashboard, printable in a click.

Payroll

Run payroll inside AUCTIX, with tax filings handled and every pay run posted straight to your books.
